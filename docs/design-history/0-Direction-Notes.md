# Paper Session: Four Directions (Phase 3 Notes)

Same content across all four PDFs so design is the only variable. Each PDF: page 1 Deep two-column react, page 2 Deep single provocation, page 3 Light rank-and-circle.

All four share the system constants: Plex Serif asks, Plex Mono reports (the machine's voice), Sans caps for infrastructure, pen answers. 50% gray floor for meaning, pages decompress downward, open territory closes every sheet.

## 1. Bureau

**Stance.** Full Swiss discipline. A heavy 1.4pt datum rule at the top of every page, flush-left everything, hairline structure, charm confined entirely to language. The sheet behaves like a well-run institution that respects your time.

**Atoms.** 1.4pt black datum rule; 0.5pt hairlines at 55% gray; ruled writing guides 0.5pt/16pt leading; 1pt answer frames; type scale 6.8/8/9.5/16/21; tracked caps labels at 40% gray.
**Molecules.** Underlined column caption; numbered empty slot (two ruled lines per slot); serif-italic intent line; rule-plus-caps open territory (no box, just a strong rule and a label).
**Organisms.** React spread with vertical "cross out freely" margin hint; full-bleed-width provocation with maximum emptiness; rank table with boxed rank, K/X circles, note rules.
**Templates.** Deep: datum header, dense-left/empty-right, decompressing to open rule. Light: same header logic one size down.

**Weakest where:** it flirts with form-smell. The discipline that makes it trustworthy for a Monday priorities session can read as cold for creative provocations, and its personality depends entirely on the writing staying sharp; with lazy microcopy it becomes a tax document.

## 2. Field Kit

**Stance.** A utilitarian instrument you'd carry, fold, and not baby. Stamped header block with a rounded 1.1pt border, dot-grid answer zones instead of ruled lines, everything labeled like equipment. The Field Notes lineage: affection through workmanlike-ness.

**Atoms.** 1.1pt rounded header box (r3); dot grid 12 to 16pt pitch, 0.5 to 0.6 radius, ~60% gray; 0.75pt rounded zone borders (r4); caps titles tracked +1.6.
**Molecules.** Stamp header (title, hairline divider, intent, date stacked inside the box); dot-grid answer slot; boxed overflow zone.
**Organisms.** React spread with dot-grid right column (invites sketching, not just writing); provocation page with a full dot-grid thinking field; rank table unchanged from system default.
**Templates.** Every page reads as a piece of kit; the box is the identity.

**Weakest where:** the dot grid is a real commitment. It prints more ink than any other direction, sits closest to the scan-legibility floor, and gently pressures neat handwriting. And the workwear register can feel too casual when the session is genuinely conceptual rather than observational.

## 3. Method Card

**Stance.** Card logic as the whole identity. Every zone is a dashed-border card with an index (Card A, B, C), corner registration ticks frame the page, and the system implicitly promises scissors: any zone could be cut out, carried, or laid on a table. The IDEO/Oblique lineage, extended to full sheets.

**Atoms.** Dashed 0.5 to 0.6pt borders (3/2.5 dash, r4-6); corner ticks; centered serif headers; card index labels; per-row card strips on Light sheets.
**Molecules.** Indexed card zone; card-strip rank row; loose card for open territory.
**Organisms.** React spread as Card A (proposal) beside Card B (arrangement); provocation as one large centered Card C; Light sheet as a stack of cut-able strips.
**Templates.** Centered header logic, cards descending, loose card closing.

**Weakest where:** fragmentation. The dashes add visual noise to every zone whether or not cutting is plausible, the centered header fights the flush-left writing hand, and on sheets that will never be cut the cut-line promise is decoration, which violates the instrument principle.

## 4. Basement Show

**Stance.** Zine conviction on a disciplined grid. A 27pt all-caps masthead over a 4pt slab rule, columns labeled THE MACHINE SAYS and YOU SAY, a standing house rule on the Light sheet ("something dies tonight, pick it"), and a footer that talks to you like a bandmate. The loudest voice; layout stays strict underneath so scanning and writing never suffer.

**Atoms.** 27pt tight-tracked caps masthead; 4pt slab rule; 1.6pt column rules; serif-bold provocations set in caps; serif-italic asides at 25-35% gray.
**Molecules.** Masthead header; shouted column captions; house-rule line; SCRIBBLE ZONE closer.
**Organisms.** React spread as an argument between two named voices; provocation page as a poster you answer; rank sheet with a standing kill rule.
**Templates.** Same grid as Bureau under the hood; only the voice and weights change.

**Weakest where:** Principle 1 risk. The masthead and slab compete with the pen for loudest-thing-on-the-page, the attitude could wear thin by the fortieth Monday, and it is the least appropriate register when the session content is heavy (a hard personal decision does not want a sassy footer).

## Recommendation

Bureau chassis, Basement voice, one Field Kit organ. Concretely: Bureau's datum rule, grid, and ruled guides as the structural system; Basement's named-voices column captions and standing house rules as the language layer, dialed to about 60%; Field Kit's dot grid reserved as an optional zone type for sketch-inviting activities only. Method Card contributes its cut-apart pattern to the card-kit sheets where cutting is real, and nothing else.

Pick a direction, name a hybrid, or redline any sheet. The winner becomes design.md.
