02The pen

Ink — amplifies

BlackNotes, context, general text. The default voice.
RedNeeds review, stop, delete, push back on this.
GreenApproved, yes, move forward.
BlueAn instruction to the AI: do this on return.

Mark — carries

strikeKill it. A considered rejection, not an absence.
circleElevate it. This matters.
?Explain or expand this on return.
!Priority.
starWorth keeping beyond this task.
→Sequence or causation. This leads to that.
@nameRoute it to a person, tool, or system.
TLDR:Treat this line as the zone’s headline answer.

The mark is the carrier and the hue is the amplifier, so a page done in one black ballpoint is the primary case rather than the fallback. Write a legend anywhere on the paper and it outranks all of this for the session.

03If the chat is cold

“The chat holds no memory of the session, so it reconstructs from what the sheet itself prints — title, date, intent line, footer numbering — and spends its one re-anchoring question there. The pages are the whole record.” README § Install, verbatim

This is why the sheets carry no QR code, no session ID, and no machine-readable block. Nothing printed on them is addressed to a machine that was already in the room.
