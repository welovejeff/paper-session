Why so much of it is empty

Most of that page is room for your pen, and the emptiness is the part that costs something to leave alone. The sheet hands you nothing to react to: no example answers, no starter list. An idea printed for you has a way of becoming the idea you spend the rest of the page arguing with, and arguing feels enough like thinking that you never notice the swap.

A sheet your assistant builds mid‑task carries your own work in the header. This one asks the question that still works on someone it has never met.
