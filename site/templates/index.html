The premise

The most useful piece of AI hardware you own might be your printer.

Generation stopped being the bottleneck a while ago. The scarce part is a person actually thinking about what came back — and that does not happen on a screen with something waiting on you.

So your AI stops mid‑task, hands you a page, and picks the work back up from your handwriting.
