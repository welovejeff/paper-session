What counts as a page

Any paper with your handwriting on it.

A printed sheet is the case the file was written around, not the requirement. A legal pad, a notebook spread, the back of an agenda. It does not have to be your handwriting either: dictated, typed, or written down by someone else at your direction all carry the authority your own hand carries.

Marks carry the intent and ink only amplifies them, so one black ballpoint is the primary case rather than the fallback.

Before you rely on it

Three things that are true about a pasted protocol, said here rather than found out later.

  1. A pasted file has no authority over the host's system prompt.

    It arrives as ordinary user text, underneath whatever the product has already told the model. Every rule in it is a request. A model tuned to be maximally helpful can still paraphrase your handwriting instead of transcribing it, reopen a judgment you settled on paper, or ask you to re-explain pages you have already sent — three things the file explicitly forbids. How often, and on which product, is unmeasured. When it happens, say so in the chat: you are the enforcement.

  2. Transcription errors arrive as fluent substitutions, not as visible garbage.

    A misread word comes back as a different, entirely plausible word. Nothing looks broken, and that is the problem — there is no ragged text to catch your eye and no confidence score to distrust. It is the reason the reply opens with a reflect-back, and the reason that paragraph is the one part of the response worth reading line by line against the page you are holding. That is the only moment a wrong reading is still cheap.

  3. Returning on a different surface starts the assistant cold.

    If one AI designed the pages and another reads them back, the second has no session to resume. It runs the orphan path: reconstruct from what is printed on the page, state plainly what the page cannot tell it, and spend its one re-anchoring question — spent on continuity rather than on your work. The authority rule that makes this whole loop worth doing, your handwriting outranking what the AI proposed, also has nothing to override in that chat, because no proposal was ever made there. Nothing breaks. You get less than a same-chat return, and it is worth knowing which of the two you are doing.
