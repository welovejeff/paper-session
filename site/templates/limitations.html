How to audit it

The brief is three tiers, and they are not equal.

Part One argues the thesis cluster by cluster. Each cluster names its sources with citation counts and links, and where the literature disagrees with itself the cluster carries a counterweight paragraph rather than dropping the disagreement.

Part Two specifies session mechanics: the clusters behind each thing a sheet is allowed to print, format by format, with contraindications.

Part Three is field reports from real sessions. It sits below everything above it by rule — a report can flag a rule for review and can never be the sole support for adding one.

Paths with no research under them at all

The empty tier

No completed session has been reported.

Part Three is where real sessions land: date, capacity, patterns used, whether the pages came back completed, partial or abandoned, and where they stopped. It has no entries. Completion is the only measure this project treats as success, and it currently has no data — which is also why there are no testimonials on this site and no percentage anywhere claiming that people finish.
