Which of these are you?

One of these sentences is true of you, and that is the whole decision. These cards are only a way in: the directions themselves are further down this page, in a single block, exactly as the repository states them, and that block is the complete and current list whether or not a card here describes your case.

  • Your AI is an app or a browser tab.

    There is somewhere to attach a file and no terminal to type into. Both halves ship as packaged bundles for exactly this. It is the shortest way in and it is not the lesser one; the ledger below says where it stands, in the same words it uses for everything else.

    The directions ↓

  • Your AI runs in a terminal or your editor.

    You have a shell, and Node with it. One installer knows this repository, finds both skills in it, and knows where your particular agent keeps them — including the ones nobody here has tested.

    The directions ↓

  • A terminal, but no Node.

    The bundles are not a special format. Whatever unpacks an archive on your machine is enough, and the destination is a folder your agent already reads.

    The directions ↓

  • Your AI cannot take skills at all.

    Nothing installs, because nothing reads a skills folder. The forward half travels as text you paste, and what comes back is a card you copy into a notebook by hand — never a PDF, and nothing verified, because nothing is rendered.

    The no-printer path →

Whichever of them you are, Python enters the picture twice: something has to draw the page, and something has to check the drawing before you ever see it. Whether either of those lands on you to install by hand depends on where your agent runs Python, not on which route you took. The directions below say what they are and why; the ledger below that says which of them you need, agent by agent.
